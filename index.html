About

I'm a Chemical Engineering PhD candidate at Louisiana State University, expected to graduate in May 2027. I work in the Process Systems Engineering group, advised by Dr. José Romagnoli and Dr. Revati Kumar.

My work combines AI-driven modeling, optimization and data science with chemical engineering, materials science and molecular simulation. I've published high-accuracy property-prediction models and I'm now building generative design tools for surfactants.

I'm looking to apply advanced modeling and data science to real industrial problems, including energy efficiency and lower-carbon solutions, in a rotational early-career research program.

Research

Machine learning on molecular graphs, grounded in molecular dynamics and aimed at design problems with limited experimental data.

Ongoing

AI-driven surfactant design

A generative AI and reinforcement learning framework that proposes new surfactant molecules. Multi-objective reward functions and property-specific graph neural network predictors target critical micelle concentration and surface tension at the same time.

Generative models, reinforcement learning, GNNs, PyTorch, RDKit

Published 2026

Predicting critical micelle concentration

Graph-based models that predict the critical micelle concentration of surfactants, comparing GCN, GAT and transformer-based architectures.

R² ≈ 0.94

Computational Materials Science

Published 2025–2026

Data augmentation for low-data regimes

Variational autoencoders generate extra molecular training data for graph neural networks. A semi-supervised extension improves surface tension prediction from small experimental datasets.

Chemical Engineering Transactions, Systems and Control Transactions

Published 2024

Ion activity in ion-exchange membranes

Graph neural networks combined with molecular-dynamics descriptors predict ion activity coefficients in polymeric ion-exchange membranes, for electrically driven separations.

R² > 0.98 on train and test sets

Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research

Ongoing

Agentic AI literature review

An LLM-agent pipeline for a systematic review of heavy-metal removal in wastewater treatment. It automates literature discovery, screening and structured data extraction.

LLM agents, LangChain, AI coding assistants
